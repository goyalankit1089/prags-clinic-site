02How we treat

Modern equipment, experienced hands

Dr. Anshu combines manual therapy, targeted exercise and modern electrotherapy — selected for your condition after a thorough assessment.

Dry Needling

Fine needles release deep trigger points that hands can't reach — certified practice.

Cupping Therapy

Certified cupping that improves blood flow, releases fascia and eases chronic tightness.

Manual Therapy

Hands-on joint mobilisation and soft-tissue release — the core of real physiotherapy.

Exercise Therapy

A personalised, progressive exercise plan so results last after the sessions end.

Ultrasound Therapy

Deep-tissue healing for inflammation, muscle spasm and chronic pain points.

IFT & TENS

Electrotherapy that calms nerve pain and relaxes muscles — safe and non-invasive.

Laser Therapy

Focused light therapy to accelerate tissue repair and reduce joint inflammation.

Shortwave Diathermy

Deep heat therapy for stiff joints, chronic backache and arthritic pain.

Post-Operative Rehab

Structured recovery programs coordinated with your surgeon's protocol.

07Good to know

Questions, answered

Do I need a doctor's referral to visit?

No referral needed. You can walk in or book directly on WhatsApp. If you have prescriptions, X-rays or MRI reports, bring them along — they help us assess faster.

What are the clinic timings?

Monday to Saturday, 10:30 AM – 2:00 PM and 5:30 – 8:00 PM. Sunday closed. Evening slots fill up fast — booking ahead on WhatsApp is recommended.

How much does a session cost?

Fees depend on your condition and treatment plan. After your first assessment, Dr. Anshu explains the plan and per-session cost clearly — before any treatment begins.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition — some patients feel relief in 3–4 sessions, chronic or post-surgical cases take longer. You'll get an honest estimate after the first assessment, and we re-evaluate progress every week.

What should I wear or bring?

Comfortable, loose clothing you can move in. Bring any previous reports, X-rays or MRI scans, and a list of medicines you take.

Where exactly is the clinic?

Shop No. 11, Ground Floor, B Block Market, Sector 62, Noida — right behind Fortis Hospital, beside Globax Pharmacy. Easy parking in the market.
